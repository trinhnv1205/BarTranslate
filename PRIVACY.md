# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 June 2026_

BarTranslate ("the app") is a macOS menu bar translator. This policy explains
what data the app handles and how. We designed BarTranslate to keep your data
on your device.

## Summary

- **No accounts.** BarTranslate does not require you to sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app does not include third-party analytics,
  advertising, or behavioral tracking SDKs.
- **Your data stays on your device** (and, if you enable it, your private
  iCloud account).

## Data the app stores locally

The following data is stored on your Mac using the standard macOS preferences
store (`UserDefaults`) and is never transmitted to us:

- **Translation history** — the source text, translated text, language pair,
  timestamps, favorite/flashcard status, and spaced-repetition progress for the
  entries you create.
- **Preferences** — hotkeys, appearance, and other settings you choose.
- **Pro entitlement** — your trial start date and, if you purchase Pro, your
  license key or purchase flag.

You can delete your history at any time from the History tab.

## iCloud sync (optional, Pro)

If you enable **iCloud sync**, your translation history is stored in your own
private iCloud key-value store so it can sync between your Macs. This data is
handled by Apple under your iCloud account and Apple's privacy terms. We have no
access to it. Disabling the setting stops syncing.

## Translation requests

Translations are performed by loading **Google Translate** inside an embedded
web view. When you translate text, that text is sent to Google in order to
produce the translation, exactly as if you used Google Translate in a browser.
Google's handling of that data is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). BarTranslate
does not add any tracking to these requests.

## Update checks (direct-download builds only)

The version downloaded outside the Mac App Store can check GitHub for a newer
release. This request contacts GitHub's public API and sends only the standard
information required to fetch the latest release (no personal data). You can
turn this off in Settings.

## Children

BarTranslate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted in
the app's release notes and in this document's "Last updated" date.
